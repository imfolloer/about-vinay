Methodology: risk identification → quantification → pathway → valuation
1
Identify findings across 8 pillars
Every DD finding belongs to one of the 8 pillars. Classify by severity: High (material deal risk), Medium (significant but manageable), Low (minor, note only). Severity determines which mitigation pathways are available.
2
Quantify the financial exposure
For each finding, estimate: (a) one-time remediation cost, (b) recurring cost impact if ongoing, (c) regulatory fine exposure, (d) probability-weighted loss. Use benchmarks from the cue library below.
3
Choose the mitigation pathway
Each finding maps to one of 6 pathways. The pathway determines whether the risk hits EBITDA (via normalisation or multiple compression) or hits price directly (via chip or escrow). Choose based on certainty, quantum, and negotiating position.
4
Build the valuation bridge
Walk: Reported EBITDA → EBITDA adjustments → Normalised EBITDA × Multiple = Gross EV → minus direct price chips → minus escrow → Net deal price. Every £1M EBITDA adjustment = £(multiple)M EV impact.
The 6 mitigation pathways — when to use each
Price chip
Certain, quantifiable, one-time cost. Deducted directly from deal price (not EBITDA). Most aggressive — seller feels full impact upfront.
Use when: known remediation cost, cyber breach, open litigation, stranded contract
Escrow
Risk is real but outcome uncertain. Funds held in trust; released to seller if risk doesn't materialise, to buyer if it does. Balances risk between parties.
Use when: regulatory investigation, conditional risk, pending cyber audit
EBITDA normalisation
Recurring cost that the target has understated or omitted. Adjusts the earnings base before applying the multiple. Maximum EV leverage — every £1M = £multiple M.
Use when: deferred maintenance, shadow IT, understated run-rate, dis-synergy
Multiple compression
Risk increases uncertainty of future earnings. Apply a lower multiple to the whole deal. Reflects elevated integration or operational risk rather than a specific cost.
Use when: open FCA investigation, COBOL mainframe, structural IT fragility
Condition of close
Risk is deal-breaker if unresolved. Require the seller to remediate before close. No financial deduction but protects buyer from inheriting the risk.
Use when: critical cyber vulnerability, open regulatory action, data breach, EOL system in prod
Note only
Risk is immaterial or already priced in. Documented in DD report but not reflected in valuation. Use sparingly — always justify why no adjustment.
Use when: minor findings, low financial exposure, risk fully mitigated by seller pre-close

Core formulae
Normalised EBITDA = Reported EBITDA − understatements + one-off add-backs Gross EV = Normalised EBITDA × EV/EBITDA multiple Net deal price = Gross EV − price chips − escrow amounts EV impact = EBITDA delta × multiple (for normalisation findings) Payback period = One-time cost ÷ annual run-rate saving Dis-synergy = standalone IT cost − embedded IT cost Stranded cost = shared IT cost × (1 − carved-out entity share) Regulatory fine = min(£17.5M, 4% of global revenue) [GDPR max UK] Tech debt proxy = deferred investment years × annual IT CapEx × 0.3–0.5
